# capital-city-provisions — Proposed AIFT OS Application Integration

> **Optional app integration research only.** The public business remains independent. No OS or hardware capability is claimed shipped. The central architecture and editable infographics are in draft [AIFT-OS PR #92](https://github.com/AIFreedomTrustFederation/AIFT-OS/pull/92).

## Source role

Independent real-world premium provisions commerce, wholesale intake, customer support, delivery and owner operations. No native OS, firmware or bootloader ownership.

## Proposed AIFT app contract

A future opt-in AIFT desktop business application may use scoped customer/owner session contracts, approved local-first reporting, and verified order/delivery operations without transferring personal data across federation nodes by default.

## Validation and milestones

1. Inspect the current repository/branch state and preserve business ownership, branding, production data boundaries and customer privacy.
2. Document app ID, source/version SHA, supported web/native targets, offline/unavailable states, permissions and export/revoke semantics.
3. Implement only after real user-authorized APIs and service contracts exist; browser mockups are not production desktop integration.
4. Verify Actual auth/role enforcement, lead/order API and browser tests, delivery/owner deny-path testing, lawful handling of customer information and explicit deployment evidence.
5. Confirm independent operator approval for external customer, financial, delivery or deployment actions.

## Non-negotiables

Do not create fictitious sales, inventory, drivers or delivery states; no automated external marketing, funds, customer contact or route actions from a docs sync. Firmware, Windows/Apple boot selection, phone flashing and autonomous robotics are not responsibilities of this business app. Follow AGENTS.md and the existing checkout's CI gates.
